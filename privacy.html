1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how RYKA (“RYKA”, “we”, “our”, or “the app”) may handle information when you use the app. The information involved depends on the features available in your version of RYKA and how you choose to use them.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on enabled features, information may include:

  • Account details you provide, such as name, username, email address, age, and phone number.
  • Account identifiers, profile information, preferences, playlists, and other data needed for features you use.
  • Technical, diagnostic, or security information generated when the app operates, if the app's implementation or SDKs collect it.

This list is a template, not a claim that every item is collected. The publisher must verify it against the released app and remove or add items as appropriate.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, provide music and app features, synchronize supported data, remember preferences, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain reliability.

4. Storage and service providers

RYKA uses Firebase Authentication for supported account functions. Depending on the features actually enabled, Firebase or other providers may process or store information needed to operate the app. Review the relevant provider policies and ensure this section accurately describes the services in the released build.

RYKA may also link to or integrate with third-party music services, APIs, websites, or content. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. RYKA does not control independent providers' privacy practices.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate enabled features or disclosed when required by applicable law. If the app uses analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other SDKs that collect or share data, describe those services here before publishing.

Do not claim that data is never shared or sold unless that statement has been verified against the app's complete data practices.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Retention and deletion

Information may be retained while needed to provide a feature, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations. To request account or data deletion, contact the developer using the contact details below. Add the exact deletion process, retention periods, and any exceptions that apply to the app before publication.

8. Children's privacy

RYKA should not collect children's personal information in a way that violates applicable law. Age requirements, parental consent, and safeguards must follow the laws and app-store rules that apply to the app and its audience.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Contact the developer to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, services, or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

Note: **Your Privacy Matters** RYKA is designed to provide a personalized music experience while respecting your privacy. For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us using the details provided in the Contact Us section below.

1. Overview

Welcome to RYKA (“RYKA”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share while using the RYKA application, website, and related services (“Services”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use RYKA, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile picture, if you choose to add one
  • Login and authentication information

Music & Usage Information

  • Songs, artists, albums, playlists and genres you interact with
  • Listening history
  • Likes, favorites and saved music
  • Playlist information
  • Music preferences and interactions with RYKA features

Device & Technical Information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from technical information
  • Crash reports, diagnostics and performance information

User-Created Content

If supported by the Services, we may process playlists, custom profile information, custom themes or UI preferences, Listen Together room information, and other content you voluntarily create or share.

These are possible categories. Actual collection depends on the features enabled and the app's configuration; verify this list before publishing.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and operate RYKA
  • Stream and manage music-related features
  • Personalize music recommendations
  • Improve AI-powered features
  • Provide AI translation features
  • Create and manage Listen Together sessions
  • Import playlists from supported third-party services when you request it
  • Maintain account security
  • Detect fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Fix bugs and improve app performance
  • Provide customer support
  • Process subscriptions and payments, where offered
  • Send important service-related notifications
  • Improve the overall RYKA experience

4. AI Features

RYKA may provide AI-powered features such as music recommendations, translations, personalization and other intelligent features.

Information necessary to provide these features may be processed by RYKA and/or trusted technology providers.

We do not intend to use your private personal information to train public AI models unless we clearly disclose this and obtain any consent required by applicable law.

5. Third-Party Music Services

RYKA may allow users to connect or import playlists from supported third-party music platforms. When you connect a third-party service, that service may provide information to RYKA according to the permissions you grant.

Your use of those third-party services is also subject to their own privacy policies and terms. RYKA does not claim ownership of music or content belonging to third-party platforms.

6. Music Rights & Copyright

RYKA respects copyright and intellectual-property rights. RYKA does not claim ownership of music, recordings, artwork, lyrics or other copyrighted content belonging to artists, labels, publishers or third-party services.

Where required, RYKA will obtain appropriate licenses, permissions or use authorized APIs/services for music-related functionality. Users must not use RYKA to illegally copy, distribute, download or redistribute copyrighted content.

7. Listen Together

RYKA may provide Listen Together or synchronized listening features. When you create or join a room, certain information such as your username, room code, playback status or other necessary session information may be processed to provide synchronization.

Do not share private or sensitive information in publicly accessible rooms or profile areas.

8. Payments & Subscriptions

RYKA may offer paid subscription plans. Payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers. RYKA may receive limited transaction information necessary to confirm your subscription, such as payment status, transaction identifier and subscription plan.

RYKA does not need to store your complete card number when payment processing is handled by an authorized payment provider.

9. Advertising

The free version of RYKA may display advertisements. Advertising partners may collect certain device or advertising information according to their own policies and applicable laws.

Premium subscribers may receive an ad-free experience where stated in their selected subscription plan.

10. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Refund & Cancellation Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for RYKA Premium subscriptions.

Last Updated: 25 October 2026

On this page Subscription Plans Cancellation Refund Requests Duplicate Charges Unauthorized Transactions Technical Problems Refund Method Non-Refundable Situations Third-Party Purchases Policy Changes Contact

Need help with a payment? If you believe you were incorrectly charged or cannot access a purchased Premium feature, contact RYKA Support with your transaction details.

1. Subscription Plans

RYKA may offer Premium plans for different durations and device limits.

Subscription prices are displayed before purchase.

2. Cancellation

You may request cancellation of your subscription according to the payment platform and subscription method used.

Cancellation does not automatically guarantee a refund.

3. Refund Requests

If you believe you were incorrectly charged, charged more than once, or experienced a significant technical problem that prevented access to a purchased service, you may contact RYKA Support.

Please provide:

  • RYKA account email
  • Transaction/order ID
  • Purchase date
  • Subscription plan
  • Description of the issue

4. Duplicate Charges

If a technical or payment error results in a verified duplicate charge for the same subscription, RYKA will investigate and, where appropriate, arrange a refund for the duplicate transaction.

5. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a transaction was unauthorized, contact RYKA as soon as possible and also contact the relevant payment provider where appropriate.

6. Technical Problems

If Premium features do not become available after a successful payment, contact support.

RYKA may investigate the account and transaction before determining an appropriate remedy.

7. Refund Method

Approved refunds will generally be returned through the original payment method or another method permitted by the payment provider.

Processing time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

8. Non-Refundable Situations

To the extent permitted by applicable law, refunds may not be available merely because:

  • You changed your mind after using the service
  • You forgot to cancel a subscription
  • You did not use the subscription
  • You used the Premium features and later decided not to continue

However, mandatory consumer rights and applicable refund requirements will always prevail.

9. Third-Party Purchases

If a subscription is purchased through a third-party app store or payment platform, additional refund rules of that platform may apply.

10. Policy Changes

RYKA may update this policy from time to time. The version applicable to your purchase will generally be the version disclosed at the relevant time, subject to applicable law.

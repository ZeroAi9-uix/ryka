Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy

RYKA respects the intellectual-property rights of artists, creators, labels, publishers and other rights holders. This policy explains how to report suspected copyright infringement involving content available through RYKA.

Last Updated: 25 October 2026

Respect creators and rights holders. Please use RYKA in ways that respect copyright and other intellectual-property rights. If you believe material available through RYKA infringes your rights, you can submit a complaint using the contact details below.

1. Copyright Respect

Users must not use RYKA to intentionally infringe copyright or other intellectual-property rights.

This includes unauthorized:

  • Copying
  • Distribution
  • Uploading
  • Public redistribution
  • Recording or extraction
  • Commercial exploitation

of copyrighted content.

2. User Content

If RYKA allows users to create or upload content, users remain responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or permissions to use that content.

3. Copyright Complaints

A copyright owner or authorized representative may contact RYKA if they believe content available through RYKA infringes their rights.

A complaint should include:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work
  • Identification or location of the allegedly infringing material
  • Contact information of the rights holder or representative
  • A statement that the complaint is made in good faith
  • A statement confirming authority to make the complaint
  • A signature or valid electronic confirmation

4. Review Process

RYKA may review copyright complaints and take appropriate action, which may include restricting or removing access to content where legally appropriate.

5. Repeat Infringement

Where appropriate and legally permitted, RYKA may restrict or terminate accounts that repeatedly engage in copyright infringement.

6. Third-Party Music

Music, artwork, metadata and other content provided through third-party services may remain subject to the rights and terms of their respective owners.

7. Contact

Copyright complaints may be sent to RYKA using the contact details below.

  • Copyright complaints: copyright@yourdomain.com

RYKA will process valid notices according to applicable law and contractual obligations.

Before publishing: Replace copyright@yourdomain.com with an official monitored email address.

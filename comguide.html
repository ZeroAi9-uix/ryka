Community Guidelines

RYKA is designed to be a positive place for discovering and enjoying music. When using social or collaborative features, please follow these guidelines.

Last Updated: 25 October 2026

On this page Don't Use RYKA To Respect Other Users Enforcement Appeals Safety

Keep RYKA respectful and safe. Help make music discovery and collaboration a positive experience for everyone. These guidelines apply when using RYKA's social and collaborative features.

1. Don't Use RYKA To

  • Harass or threaten others
  • Bully or target users
  • Impersonate people or organizations
  • Share private personal information
  • Spread malicious or fraudulent content
  • Upload content that violates applicable law
  • Infringe copyright
  • Abuse reporting systems
  • Spam other users
  • Attempt to compromise another user's account
  • Disrupt Listen Together rooms or other services

2. Respect Other Users

Treat other users with respect.

Do not use RYKA's communication or social features to repeatedly disturb, intimidate or target another person.

3. Enforcement

Depending on the seriousness of a violation, RYKA may:

  • Remove content
  • Restrict a feature
  • Restrict a room
  • Temporarily suspend an account
  • Permanently terminate an account
  • Report unlawful activity where legally required

4. Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was made incorrectly, contact RYKA Support:

  • Email: support@yourdomain.com

We may review the decision where appropriate.

5. Safety

Do not share sensitive personal information publicly.

If you encounter suspicious activity, report it to RYKA Support.

Support: support@yourdomain.com

Before publishing: Replace support@yourdomain.com with your official monitored support email address.
